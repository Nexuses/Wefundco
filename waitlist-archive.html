Before you ask

The four questions
everybody asks

Will my pitch deck get shared without my knowledge?

Never. There is nowhere to upload a deck. Your verified profile is what an investor sees, and no introduction is made without your go ahead. Nothing leaves WeFundCo because somebody found your email address.

What does it cost?

Free for the founding cohort of startups. Free for the first 100 verified investors. Pricing for members after that is published before it applies, and waitlist members hear it first.

I am not in a metro. Does that count against me?

No. Geography is a filter investors can use, not a gate that excludes you. If you are building in Coimbatore, Indore, Lucknow or anywhere else and the business is strong, you belong here. Sector, stage and traction are what matter.

Is the platform live yet?

Not yet. We are in pre-launch and assembling the founding cohort. Everyone on the waitlist is notified first, and founding members get first access and first visibility. We would rather launch late than introduce you into an empty room.
