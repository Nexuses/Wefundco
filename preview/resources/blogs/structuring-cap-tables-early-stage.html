Structuring Cap Tables for Early-Stage Diligence

Cap tables tell a story about how a company has raised, incentivised and aligned its stakeholders. When that story is fragmented across spreadsheets, side letters and informal promises, diligence slows down — and trust erodes.

Start with a single source of truth

Every issuance, transfer, option grant and convertible instrument should roll up to one reconciled view. Founders should be able to explain not only current ownership percentages, but the sequence of events that produced them.

Common friction points in diligence

  • Unclear option pool sizing and refresh assumptions
  • Convertible notes with varying discount or cap terms
  • Informal advisor equity without documented grants

Presentation matters

Investors rarely need exotic modelling on day one. They need clarity: fully diluted ownership, key rights, and any material restrictions on future rounds. A clean export — paired with underlying agreements — beats a complex workbook investors cannot audit quickly.
