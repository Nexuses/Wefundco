Understanding Employment Contracts: Rights, Restrictions, and Remedies

As teams grow, employment contracts stop being administrative paperwork and become part of the company’s governance story. Investors often review how a business hires, incentivises and protects its people — not only what the cap table says.

A clear contract framework reduces ambiguity for employees and gives leadership a consistent basis for decisions when roles, equity or departures are in play.

Rights founders should document clearly

Compensation structure, role scope, leave policies and IP assignment should be explicit. Ambiguity in any of these areas tends to surface later — sometimes during diligence, sometimes during a departure.

Restrictions that require careful drafting

Non-compete, non-solicit and confidentiality clauses vary in enforceability. The goal is protection without overreach that creates future disputes or reputational friction with talent markets you may need again.

Remedies when relationships end

Notice periods, garden leave, acceleration triggers and return-of-property clauses should align with your broader shareholder and option agreements. Misalignment here is a common source of last-minute diligence questions.
