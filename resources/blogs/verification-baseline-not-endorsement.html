Why Verification Is a Baseline — Not an Endorsement

Verification exists to improve the quality of discovery — not to replace the judgement investors bring to their own mandates, diligence processes and investment decisions.

On WeFundCo, verification is intended to support authenticity and structured participation. It helps participants know that basic identity and credibility checks have been applied before meaningful engagement begins.

What verification is designed to do

  • Reduce anonymous or misaligned participation in a private capital context
  • Support clearer, more structured profiles and documentation
  • Create a consistent entry standard for platform members

What verification does not do

It does not certify investment merit, guarantee performance or absolve investors of independent assessment. A verified profile is a starting point for evaluation — not a recommendation to invest.

That distinction matters for founders and investors alike. It keeps the platform honest about its role: infrastructure for readiness and discovery, not a substitute for professional advice or transaction responsibility.
