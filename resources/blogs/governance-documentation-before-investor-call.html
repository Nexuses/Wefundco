Governance Documentation Before the First Investor Call

The first investor conversation is rarely only about the pitch. It is often the moment when structured questions begin: who owns what, how decisions are recorded, and whether the company’s legal foundation matches the story on the slide.

Governance documentation is not bureaucracy for its own sake. It is the evidence that a business takes accountability, continuity and capital discipline seriously.

Corporate records investors expect to exist

  • Certificate of incorporation and constitutional documents
  • Board and shareholder resolutions for key actions
  • Register of directors, charges and statutory filings where applicable

Cap table and equity documentation

Issuance history, option pools, convertible instruments and side letters should reconcile to a single source of truth. Gaps here slow diligence and can reshape term sheet discussions unexpectedly.

Before you book the call

Founders do not need perfection — but they do need coherence. A concise data room index, even if some folders are marked “in progress,” signals preparedness better than ad hoc file sharing after the meeting.
