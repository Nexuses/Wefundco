Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CapitalPrime Technologies Private Limited ("WeFundCo", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you use wefundco.com and www.wefundco.com, including our waitlist and onboarding forms (the "Site"). We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. Information we collect

  • Waitlist details: your email address, optional phone number, your role (for example startup, investor or partner), and the page and source you signed up from.
  • Onboarding details: the contact and business information you enter in the onboarding form, and any documents or files you upload.
  • Technical data: browser and device information (such as your user agent), pages viewed and approximate usage data collected through analytics and cookies.
  • Correspondence: anything you send us by email or through social platforms.

2. How we use your information

  • to manage the waitlist and contact you about early access and platform updates;
  • to review onboarding submissions and prepare for conversations with you;
  • to operate, secure and improve the Site and prevent misuse; and
  • to meet legal and regulatory obligations.

We rely on your consent, which you give when you submit a form, and on our legitimate interest in running and securing the Site. You can withdraw consent at any time (see "Your rights" below).

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the Site, under appropriate confidentiality and security terms. These include providers of website hosting, database and file storage, analytics and email delivery. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Cookies and analytics

The Site uses analytics tools, including Google Analytics and Vercel Analytics, to understand how visitors use it. These tools may set cookies or use similar technologies. We also use a strictly necessary session cookie for the private admin area. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking some cookies may affect how the Site works.

5. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required by law. When it is no longer needed we delete or anonymise it.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards to protect personal information, including access controls and encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, to withdraw your consent, or to unsubscribe from our communications. You can also raise a grievance with us. To do any of this, email investments@wefundco.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

8. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Third-party links

The Site links to third-party websites and social platforms, such as LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Instagram and Facebook. Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to read their policies.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it last changed. Please review it periodically.
