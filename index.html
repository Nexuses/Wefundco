The problem

FAQ

Straight answers to the
questions worth asking

Does joining the waitlist guarantee access?

No. It is the first step. After joining, you will be invited to complete a verified profile. Access is granted only after review. Not everyone who applies will be onboarded.

What does verification involve?

For startups: an incorporated entity, a real team, and claims supported by documentation. For investors: verified identity and active deployment. Most reviews are completed within a few business days.

Will my pitch deck get shared without my knowledge?

Never. Nothing is shared without your approval. You decide whether to connect, when, and with whom.

What does it cost?

Free for the founding cohort of startups. Free for the first 100 verified investors. Pricing for subsequent members will be announced at launch.

Is WeFundCo only for metro startups?

Definitely not. If you are building in Coimbatore, Indore, Lucknow or anywhere else in India and your business is strong, you belong here. Sector, stage and traction are what matter. Not your postcode.

Is the platform live yet?

Not yet. We are in pre-launch. When we go live, everyone on the waitlist is notified first. Founding cohort members get first access and first visibility.

Is WeFundCo a broker or an adviser?

No. WeFundCo is a discovery and introduction platform. It does not raise capital, execute transactions or give investment advice. Every decision stays with you.
