FAQ

Questions worth
answering upfront

Is WeFundCo live yet?

Not yet. We're in pre-launch and building the founding cohort. Joining the waitlist reserves your place, gets you early access when matching goes live, and locks in founding-cohort benefits.

How does matching actually work?

It isn't a black box. Investors state sectors, stages, typical ticket size and geography. Startups complete a structured profile against the same parameters. We match on those stated signals, nothing hidden, nothing inferred.

Do investors receive unsolicited pitch decks?

No. You see structured profile data first and decide whether to connect. Nothing lands in your inbox because a founder found your email.

What does verification involve?

Every startup goes through a basic verification before appearing to investors, entity, founding team and the core claims on the profile. It is a floor for credibility, not an endorsement or a recommendation to invest.

What does it cost?

Access is free for the first 100 investors. Pricing for later cohorts and for startups will be published before launch, waitlist members hear first.

Is WeFundCo a broker or an advisor?

No. WeFundCo is a discovery and matching platform. We do not raise capital, execute transactions, or give investment advice. Every decision stays with you.
