FAQ

Questions worth
answering upfront

Is WeFundCo live yet?

Not yet. We're in pre-launch and building the founding cohort. Joining the waitlist reserves your place, gets you early access when matching goes live, and locks in founding-cohort benefits.

How does matching actually work?

It isn't a black box. Startups complete a structured profile covering sector, stage, typical ticket size and geography. We surface you on those stated signals, nothing hidden, nothing inferred.

Will my pitch deck go out unsolicited?

No. Your structured profile is what gets seen first. Reaching out stays your call. Nothing leaves WeFundCo because someone found your email.

What does verification involve?

Every startup goes through a basic verification before going live: entity, founding team and the core claims on the profile. It is a floor for credibility, not an endorsement.

What does it cost?

Access is free for the founding startup cohort. Pricing for later cohorts will be published before launch. Waitlist members hear first.

Is WeFundCo a broker or an advisor?

No. WeFundCo is a discovery and matching platform. We do not raise capital, execute transactions, or give investment advice. Every decision stays with you.
