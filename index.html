FAQ

What is WeFundCo?

WeFundCo is an investment readiness, intelligence and transaction platform designed to help businesses prepare for capital and enable investors to evaluate opportunities with greater clarity before deeper engagement.

Who is WeFundCo for?

WeFundCo is designed for startups, growth-stage businesses, investors and specialist partners participating across the investment and transaction journey.

How does WeFundCo help businesses become investment-ready?

WeFundCo helps businesses organise key information, identify readiness gaps and prepare across areas such as financials, valuation, ownership, compliance and diligence preparedness.

How does WeFundCo support investors?

WeFundCo brings together structured company information, readiness indicators, valuation context and risk visibility to support better-informed initial evaluation before deeper diligence.

Does WeFundCo guarantee funding or investment?

No. WeFundCo supports readiness, discovery and informed evaluation, but does not guarantee funding, investment or transaction completion. Participants remain responsible for their own decisions, diligence and professional advice.

Does AI-assisted diligence replace professional due diligence?

No. AI-assisted tools may help structure information and surface areas for further review. Independent legal, financial, tax and specialist diligence remains essential where required.

How are businesses and investors connected?

Discovery is based on relevant factors such as sector, stage, ticket size, geography and investment criteria, helping surface more relevant opportunities and connections. It does not constitute an investment recommendation.

WeFundCo uses technology and AI to bring structure and efficiency to discovery, readiness and evaluation. Specialist and human expertise remains central where it matters most: in transaction judgement, negotiation and execution.
