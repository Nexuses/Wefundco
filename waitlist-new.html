Frequently asked questions

Before you apply

What is the WeFundCo founding startup cohort?

The founding cohort is the first group of verified startups on WeFundCo. Members get priority visibility when investor matching goes live, a permanent Founding Cohort designation, input into the verification framework and no platform fee during the founding period.

Who is eligible?

You must be an incorporated Indian company with valid legal documentation. The platform is for startups that are raising or expect to raise within six months. Pre-incorporation teams should return once registered. Joining the waitlist does not guarantee platform access. Every profile is reviewed.

Is joining the waitlist the same as getting on the platform?

No. Anyone can join the waitlist. Access requires building a verified profile and passing review. We would rather tell you that here than after you have filled in fourteen fields.

What does it cost?

Free for the founding startup cohort during the pre-launch period. Pricing for members after that will be published before it applies. Waitlist members hear about it first.

Will my pitch deck be shared without my knowledge?

Never. There is nowhere to upload a deck on the waitlist. Your verified profile is what an investor sees, and no introduction is made without your explicit go-ahead.

I am not in a metro. Does that count against me?

No. Geography is a filter investors can use, not a gate that excludes you. If you are building in Coimbatore, Indore, Lucknow or anywhere else and the business is strong, you belong here.
