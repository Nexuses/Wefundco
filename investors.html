01 / The signal problem

High volume. Low relevance.

Inbound deal flow in India skews toward the same sectors, the same cities and the same circles. Founders outside the accelerator network rarely reach investors who are actually the right fit.

  • Decks that do not match your stage
  • Founders you were not looking for

Trust and control

How matching works.
And what it does not do.

You set the thesis.

The platform filters to it, nothing more. A fintech-focused pre-seed investor only sees fintech startups at pre-seed. Matching never overrides your parameters or pushes deals outside them.

You are never pushed toward a connection.

Matching surfaces an opportunity; connecting is always your decision. WeFundCo does not introduce you to a founder without your explicit action.

Your information stays private until you say otherwise.

Startups can see that you exist and that you invest in their category. They do not see your contact details until you initiate a connection.

Verification is a baseline, not an endorsement.

The WeFundCo Verified system confirms a startup is what it says it is, nothing more. It does not make investment recommendations. All diligence and investment decisions remain entirely with you.
