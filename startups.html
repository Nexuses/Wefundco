The problem

A great business should not need a great contact list.

You have built something real. The business model works. The traction is there. But fundraising in India still runs largely on who introduces you, which cohort you were in, which network you belong to.

That is a structural problem, not a you problem. And it has a structural solution.

What founders run into most often:

  • Investor lists that are guarded, outdated or sold by platforms with no lead qualification
  • Cold outreach that goes nowhere because there is no context before the first message
  • Demo days where you are one of forty startups in a room of generalist investors
  • Platforms that list your startup alongside thousands of others with no genuine discovery layer
  • Feedback loops that tell you nothing about why an investor passed

WeFundCo replaces the contact list with a verified profile. Your business does the qualifying, not your network.

Your WeFundCo profile

What investors see before
they decide to connect.

Your profile is not a pitch deck. It is a structured, verified record across six dimensions:

Entity and registration

Company type, incorporation date, registered address, CIN/GSTIN, DPIIT status where applicable.

Founding team

Founder backgrounds, roles and verification of identity. Investors see who they are talking to before the first meeting.

Business model

Sector, sub-sector, business model type, revenue model and stage. Structured, not self-described.

Traction indicators

Revenue, user/customer metrics, growth rates. You choose what to share. Verified claims are marked as verified.

Funding history

Previous rounds, current raise details, use of funds. No vague “raising X crore” without supporting context.

Governance and documentation

Cap table status, shareholder agreements, compliance records. This is what the verification levels reflect.
