Event types

Four formats. One standard.
Every attendee is verified.

Roundtable

Closed-door sessions on what actually gets funded

Investors host or join discussions on sector trends, deal quality and diligence standards. Participation is by invitation within the verified community. No pitch decks. No founders in the room unless explicitly included.

Pitch Day

Sector-specific pitch sessions

Verified startups present to an investor panel already aligned with their stage and sector. Startups are matched to the investor audience before the session begins. Not an open call. Not a general demo day.

Founder Workshop

Practical sessions for operators

Led by verified ecosystem partners and experienced operators. Topics: fundraising readiness, governance basics, financial modelling for early-stage companies, investor relations and term sheet navigation. Applied content, not introductory theory.

Ecosystem Connect

Contextual partner introductions

Structured introductions between startups and Growth Network partners at the moment the introduction is actually relevant. A startup building a verified profile connects with a legal partner when it reaches governance documentation. Not a cold referral. A contextual introduction.

How it works

Structured. Verified.
Followed up.

  1. 01

    Verified attendance

    Every attendee is a verified WeFundCo platform member or a vetted guest of one. There is no open registration.

  2. 02

    Matched rooms

    For Pitch Days and Roundtables, investor-startup matching determines the invite list. A growth-stage investor is not in the room for a pre-seed pitch day.

  3. 03

    Structured formats

    Sessions follow defined agendas. Presentations have standard formats. Q&A is moderated. Time is protected.

  4. 04

    Post-event continuity

    Introductions made at WeFundCo events are logged on the platform. The relationship does not end when the event does.

Host an event

Investors and ecosystem partners can host on WeFundCo.

Verified investors on the platform can apply to host their own roundtable or pitch session. WeFundCo supports the format, the invite management and the post-event follow-through.

  • Access to a pre-verified attendee base across startups and investors
  • Platform tools for invitation, agenda and confirmation management
  • Co-branding with the WeFundCo event identity
  • Post-event introduction logging so relationships have continuity
